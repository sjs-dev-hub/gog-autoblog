## How the guides are made

Guild uses OpenAI models within a structured editorial workflow to produce and improve guide drafts. Automation does not lower the publishing standard: claims must stay within approved evidence, drafts must pass completeness and duplication checks, and the system is prohibited from inventing hands-on testing, prices, specifications, reviews, scarcity, or personal experience.

The goal is not to replace a qualified coach, fitter, or your own observation. It is to give you a clearer question, a practical comparison, and a useful next step.

## Shopping links and editorial independence

Some guides include Amazon shopping links after the relevant decision criteria. If you buy through those links, Guild of Golf may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That relationship does not change the evidence rules or justify recommending an unsuitable product.

As an Amazon Associate, Guild of Golf earns from qualifying purchases.
